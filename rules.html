Overview

Nest Run is an asymmetric multiplayer game where Shooters escort a rail cart along a winding track to an alien nest, while a Commander spawns alien bugs and deploys traps to stop them.

Shooter Win Condition

Push the cart all the way to the nest before time runs out.

Commander Win Condition

Run out the 10-minute clock before the cart reaches the nest.

Game Modes

PvP — Shooters vs Commander

1–4 Shooters face off against 1 Commander. The Commander spawns bugs, places walls, and activates traps using biomass. A 10-minute timer counts down — if the cart hasn't reached the nest when time expires, the Commander wins.

Players1–4 Shooters + 1 Commander
Time Limit10:00
Starting Biomass100 × shooter count

Co-op

2–4 Shooters work together with no human Commander. An AI Commander spawns walls on the track and activates egg sac traps as the cart progresses. Enemy bugs spawn automatically with scaling difficulty.

Solo

Single player mode. Same as Co-op but you're on your own. Bug difficulty scales with deaths and cart progress.

Shooter Guide

The Cart

Stay close to the cart to push it forward along the track. If you die or move away, the cart slows down. Walls placed on the track block the cart — destroy them to keep moving.

Weapons

SMG — Primary

Full-auto rifle with a laser sight. Your bread and butter for clearing bugs.

Damage36 per shot
Headshot56 (1.55×)
Fire Rate11.2 rounds/sec
Range280 units

Force Gun — Secondary (unlocked at 25% progress)

A short-range push cannon that knocks enemies back. Unlocked at the checkpoint marker on the track. Press Q or Y to swap weapons.

Push Force60 units/sec
Fire Rate1.5 shots/sec
Range54 units

Health & Respawning

You have 200 HP. When killed, you respawn at the start after a 3-second delay. Each death makes enemy bugs slightly stronger and faster, so try to stay alive.

Spawn Safe Zone

The area around the spawn point is a safe zone — enemies can't damage you there. Use it to regroup after a death.

Traps

Egg sacs near the track can be activated as traps. When you walk into an active trap's radius, you'll be yanked toward the egg sac and held for 5 seconds, taking 100 damage total. Your weapons are disabled while trapped.

You can shoot egg sacs to destroy them before they catch you (200 HP, about 6 SMG shots). In co-op, teammates can also shoot the egg sac to free you.

Commander Guide

As Commander, you have a top-down view of the entire map. You spend biomass to spawn bugs, place walls, and activate traps to prevent the cart from reaching the nest.

Biomass

Your resource for everything. It regenerates over time and scales with the number of shooters for fairness.

Starting100 × shooter count
Max200 × shooter count
Regen8/sec × shooter count
Checkpoint Bonus+100 × shooter count at 25%

Spawning Bugs

Left-click anywhere on the map to spawn an active bug that immediately chases the nearest player. Right-click to spawn a dormant bug that stands still until a shooter gets within 15 units or you click to wake it manually. Great for ambushes.

Map Markers

  • Green/Blue/Yellow/Orange dots — Shooter players (with direction indicators)
  • Red dots — Active enemy bugs
  • Yellow rings + "DORMANT" label — Dormant bugs with wake radius
  • Brown rectangles — Nest walls on the track
  • Blue ring — Force gun checkpoint (25% progress)

Enemies

Basic Bug

Standard alien that chases the nearest player. Can jump when close. Cheap and plentiful.

Cost20 biomass
HP70
Speed6 units/sec
Cooldown0.5 sec

Acid Bug

Faster but more fragile. When killed, releases an acid cloud that blinds nearby shooters for 6 seconds.

Cost60 biomass
HP50
Speed7 units/sec
Blind Radius10 units
Blind Duration6 seconds
Cooldown15 sec

Dormant Bug

Any bug type spawned with right-click. Stands still playing idle animation until a shooter enters its 15-unit wake radius or the Commander clicks to wake it. Same stats as the base type once active.

Commander Abilities

Nest Wall — Key 3

Place an organic wall across the track to block the cart. Shooters must destroy it to continue. Click on the track to place.

Cost80 biomass
HP1,200
Cooldown25 sec
Size10w × 4h × 2d

Egg Sac Trap — Click egg sac

Click an egg sac on the map to arm it as a trap. When a shooter walks within range, they're yanked toward it and held for 5 seconds while taking damage. Limited to 3 activations per match.

Cost40 biomass
Max Per Match3
Catch Radius40 units
Hold Duration5 sec
Damage100 total
Trap HP200 (shootable)

Speed Boost — Key 4

All bugs move at 2× speed for 20 seconds. Use it when you have a swarm in position.

CostFree
Uses2 per match
Duration20 sec

Tips & Strategy

Shooter Tips

  • Stay near the cart — it only moves when you're close.
  • Prioritize acid bugs. They're fragile but the blind effect is devastating.
  • Watch for dormant bugs — they'll ambush you if you walk past without looking.
  • Shoot egg sacs before they catch you. Listen for the activation and look for glowing sacs ahead.
  • Use the Force Gun to knock groups of bugs off the track — it buys time even if it doesn't kill.
  • In co-op, one player can push while others clear the path ahead.
  • Each death makes bugs stronger. Play cautiously early and save aggression for when you have the Force Gun.

Commander Tips

  • Don't spend all your biomass at once — it regenerates, so maintain steady pressure.
  • Place walls just ahead of the cart to force shooters to stop and fight.
  • Dormant bugs are great for ambushes at chokepoints. Place them around corners.
  • Use acid bugs near walls — the blind effect on death makes it harder to shoot the wall down.
  • Save your 3 trap activations for the later half of the track when it matters most.
  • Time your speed boosts when you have a large swarm near the shooters.
  • The checkpoint at 25% gives you a biomass bonus too — spend it on a wall + bug combo.
